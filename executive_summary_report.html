Key Analytical Findings

A notable 88.5% of transactions conclude on Day 0 (the initial visit), confirming an impulse-driven purchase behavior. The primary systemic bottleneck across paid campaigns occurs at checkout initiation: steep attrition results in only 23.5% of checkout sessions converting into completed orders. Consequently, overall end-to-end funnel conversion (CR) ranges between 0.4% and 2.5%.

Comparison Engine, Search, PMax | DiamondTools

Core Revenue Drivers & Closing Efficiency

Comparison Engine / Referral

Primary profitability driver (105 711.99 PLN revenue, ROAS 12.31, AOV 1 215.08 PLN). Full attribution parity (100% Match) with strong conversion efficiency (CR 2.27%). Blends fast checkout completion (72.7% Day 0) with a price comparison role (24 deferred transactions).

PowerBrand – ElectroStore – Search Website

Second largest revenue contributor (43 398.90 PLN, ROAS 3.63). Direct conversion characteristics (73.5% Day 0, Match 90.7%). Requires on-site CRO to compress high acquisition costs (CPA 265.86 PLN) and lift conversion rates (CR 1.04%).

PMax | DiamondTools

High-converting, stable channel (ROAS 3.65, CR 2.98%, low CPA 100.51 PLN). Primed for budget scaling provided average order value (AOV) is systematically raised via cross-selling.

PowerBrand-Shopping, Micro-Campaigns

Unprofitable Spend & Traffic Inefficiencies

PowerBrand-Shopping

Highest aggregate ad spend (12 921.38 PLN) yielding critical underperformance (ROAS = 1.08, CPA = 208.41 PLN) with a poor 0.45% CR. While capturing 13 783 users (>50% of aggregate traffic), only 0.4% convert end-to-end.

Micro-Campaigns (adbox_comparison, DiamondTools discount, aku, cross-network)

Negligible transaction volume, negative ROI, and steep acquisition costs for cross-network (CPA 296.00 PLN), generating net capital drag.

Strategic Recommendations

Tactical initiatives covering paid media reallocation, on-site conversion optimization, CRM lifecycle workflows, and organic visibility.

1. Paid Media Budget Allocation

Scale Capital Efficiency & Eliminate Waste

- Fund Comparison Engine Liquidity: Maintain active pre-paid balances and lift CPC caps on high-margin catalog items to prevent delivery interruptions.

- Curb Inefficient Spend: Cut PowerBrand-Shopping ad spend by at least 50% and pause zero-conversion micro-campaigns (adbox_comparison, DiamondTools discount, aku). Reallocate budget toward Comparison Engine and PMax DiamondTools.

- PMax DiamondTools Strategy: Scale budget incrementally while introducing catalog bundling and cross-sell modules on product pages to raise AOV.

2. Store Experience & Checkout CRO

Eliminate Conversion Bottlenecks

- Frictionless Checkout: Remove mandatory account registration, enable one-click wallets (BLIK, Apple Pay, Google Pay), and display real-time dispatch countdowns to lift checkout completion toward 50%.

- Homepage Layout & A/B Testing: Downsize hero banners, implement a top-deal product carousel, and integrate structured technical specification tables and FAQ sections on product detail pages.

3. Retargeting & Cart Recovery

Capture Extended Decision Windows

- Automated Cart Recovery: Trigger automated email/push notifications for abandoned checkouts alongside time-sensitive incentives (e.g., 60-minute vouchers) to secure immediate purchase completion.

- Direct Customer Retention: Implement tiered B2B pricing models, targeted newsletters, and 'Reorder Now' triggers for returning customers.

- Dynamic Remarketing (7-Day Window): Limit retargeting windows to 7 days to eliminate ad spend waste beyond active purchase consideration.

4. SEO, AI Readiness & Data Quality

Zero-Cost Visibility & Tracking Accuracy

- SEO & Schema.org Rich Snippets: Strengthen organic visibility for PowerBrand and DiamondTools brand keywords and implement Schema markup for search engine and generative AI discovery.

- Referral Growth: Secure backlink placements across official manufacturer directories ('Where to Buy / Authorized Dealers') and industry networks.

- Resolve '(not set)' Traffic: Perform complete UTM tag auditing across active campaigns to eliminate attribution gaps.
